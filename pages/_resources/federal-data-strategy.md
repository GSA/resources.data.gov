  ### What the Federal Data Strategy was


  The Federal Data Strategy was a government-wide framework for how agencies manage and use data. It grew out of a March 2018 President's Management Agenda Cross-Agency Priority Goal, Leveraging Data as a Strategic Asset, and was formally established by [OMB Memorandum M-19-18](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/M-19-18.pdf) in June 2019.


  Its mission was to fully leverage the value of federal data for mission, service, and the public good by guiding the Federal Government in practicing ethical governance, conscious design, and a learning culture.

  ### How it relates to current policy


  The Federal Data Strategy is not current policy and should not be cited as a current requirement. Agencies looking for current direction on data inventories and metadata should start with OMB M-25-05 and the [DCAT-US Schema v3.0](../dcat-us3/) reference.


  Several resources on this site were produced under the Strategy and remain in active use, including the Data Ethics Framework, the Data Governance Playbook, and the Curated Data Skills Catalog. Browse them under the [Federal Data Strategy keyword](/keywords/federal-data-strategy/).


  ### Where the full record is preserved


  The Strategy had its own website at strategy.data.gov, which was retired in 2026. Nothing from it was discarded. The complete site, including every news item, the agency proof point case studies, the action progress reporting, and all published documents, is preserved in two places that will remain publicly available.
